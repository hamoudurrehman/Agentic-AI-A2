# GigSim & Upwork Marketplace Policy Pack

## 1. No Off-Platform Contact or Payment
- Freelancers and clients must keep all communications and payments on-platform prior to an active, compliant contract.
- Sharing of email addresses, phone numbers, Skype/Telegram/WhatsApp handles, external payment links, crypto wallets, or bank wires is strictly prohibited.
- Solicitations for off-platform payment must be reported immediately and rejected.

## 2. No Fabricated Experience or False Credentials
- All claims of past projects, client outcomes, technologies used, metrics achieved, or company affiliations must be verifiably grounded in the freelancer's portfolio.
- Fabricating portfolio items, exaggerating responsibilities, or claiming unverified skills violates platform integrity standards.

## 3. No Unrealistic Guarantees
- Freelancers must not make absolute guarantees (e.g., "100% guaranteed #1 Google rank", "guaranteed 10x revenue increase", "zero-risk profit").
- Realistic technical commitments and project milestones are allowed.

## 4. Fair Use of Automation and Human Supervision
- Automated scraping, rapid batch bot bidding, spamming generic proposals, and unmonitored messaging are prohibited.
- All proposals and client messages must be reviewed, personalized, and confirmed by a human freelancer prior to dispatch.
